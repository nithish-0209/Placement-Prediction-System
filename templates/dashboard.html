Feature Scaling: StandardScaler vs MinMaxScaler (M2)

Comparing mathematical properties and algorithmic necessity:

Method Mathematical Formula When it Matters When it Doesn't
StandardScaler z = (x - μ) / σ Gradient Descent (SGD, Logistic), PCA, KMeans Decision Trees, Random Forest, XGBoost
MinMaxScaler z = (x - min) / (max - min) Bounded intervals [0, 1], Image pixels Outlier-rich datasets (compresses inliers)
Why Trees don't care: Decision trees split on rank ordering ($x_j \ge \theta$), which is invariant under any strictly monotonic transformation. Linear models, however, experience distorted elliptical loss contours without scaling, crippling gradient descent convergence.

Unsupervised Feature Engineering (M4 Practical Pipeline)

Feeding unsupervised clustering representations into downstream supervised models:

  • 1
    One-Hot Encoding: Nominal streams (CS, IT, Mech, ECE, Civil, Elec) & college tiers transformed into binary dummy columns with handle_unknown='ignore'.
  • 2
    MissingIndicator Injection: Boolean masks generated for missing fields before median replacement.
  • 3
    K-Means Latent Archetypes: Students mapped to $K=4$ academic archetype centroids. Continuous euclidean distances cluster_dist_0..3 and discrete cluster_label appended directly as supervised features!
Skew-Free Artifact: Everything is serialized inside a single pipeline.joblib artifact, guaranteeing that the exact same transformations apply identically during inference as during training.
